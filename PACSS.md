Actually they're the statistics of the player

Power (Amount of force a player can hit the ball with)
Speed (Speed of the player)
Dexterity (Amount of time for the player to kick the ball again)
Control (Maximum distance from which the player may be able to kick the ball)
Strength (Probability of a player to win a duel/match against another player)

When creating a player, one must decide where to assign 10 PACSS points. A minimum of 3 will be required.
