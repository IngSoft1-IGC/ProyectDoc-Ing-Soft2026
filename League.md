A league is a collection of events, teams and matches played or yet to be played.

Leagues may be created by submitting a form providing:
1. Name
2. Match duration <= 5 min or equivalent in ticks
3. Minimum of teams required to join >= 3 teams
4. Maximum of teams permitted to join.
5. Password (optional)

Each league has its own local leaderboard, tracking statistics for each participant team:
1. Team name
2. Owner (User that created the team)
3. Wins
4. Losses
5. Total Goals

Within a league, all teams must play matches against each other.
A list of open and private leagues leagues must be shown. 
Users may be able to play friendly matches against each other at any given time.
